4 · Astrology + Abjad Lab

V8 automatically uses every stadium, referee, captain, coach and kit-colour field returned by the match feed. It also checks common alternate field names. Abjad uses the English team name when available, then transliterates it approximately. Details not provided by any connected source remain marked as missing; they are never guessed.

Astrology and Abjad are experimental entertainment methods, not scientifically validated ways to predict football. Their picks are heuristic and must be evaluated against actual results; no method guarantees success.

How the model works

The model blends each team's recent scoring and conceding rates with competition averages, applies a small home advantage, and uses a Poisson score matrix to estimate 1X2, likely scores, Over/Under 2.5 and Both Teams To Score. Confidence describes data coverage and sample size — it is not the probability that a pick will win. The model does not currently use confirmed lineups, injuries, tactics, weather or bookmaker odds.

Predictions are statistical estimates, not guaranteed results. Exact-score probabilities are naturally much lower than 1X2 probabilities. Demo mode uses fictional sample teams and is not live data.
